## Pitfalls in the stencil
- lab5's `FeatureTracker` defaults to `pause_on_display_ = true` and would block on every
  frame. The shim now calls `setOutput(false, ...)`.
- The first callback matched a frame with itself, and `prev_pose_` was still the
  zero quaternion. The first frame is now only stored.
- The latest odometry message is not the pose at the image time. GT poses are buffered
  and interpolated (lerp + slerp) at the RGB stamp. Image pairs are queued until
  odometry newer than their stamp has arrived, which also means no frame is dropped
  when SIFT is slower than the bag.
- A failed RANSAC leaves the identity, whose zero translation made `scaleTranslation`
  divide by zero. Scaling is skipped for invalid estimates, and they are flagged
  in the csv.
- OpenGV's E satisfies f1^T E f2 = 0, while OpenCV's `recoverPose` assumes x2^T E x1 = 0.
  For the non-RANSAC path, `extractPose(Es, pts2, pts1, ...)` therefore returns R12 and
  t12 directly.
- **OpenGV hang (patched).** With a tight threshold (0.1 px) and 5000 iterations, the 5-point
  RANSAC hung forever on frame 167. Replaying that frame offline isolated a 5-point sample
  on which `fivept_nister` never returns (gdb: `math::Sturm::bracketRoots`).
  `Bracket::dividable` keeps bisecting any bracket with ≥ 2 roots. With a repeated or
  near-repeated root, or with a `size_t` root count that underflowed because round-off made
  the Sturm sign counts inconsistent, the bracket shrinks to one ulp, its midpoint equals
  an endpoint, and the loop never ends. About 1 sample in 10^6 triggers it.
  `dependencies/opengv_sturm_terminate.patch` stops bisection when the bracket is narrower
  than eps or the midpoint no longer moves; `build_workspace.sh` applies it. After the patch,
  3.6 M samples on the same frame, including the seeds that hung before, all complete.
- Shell pitfall: `pkill -f pose_estimation` or `pkill -f "rosbag play"` also matches the
  calling bash command line and kills it. Stop processes by PID or with `pkill -x`.
